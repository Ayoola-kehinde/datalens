1Color System

A Signal Blue + Terminal Slate system in the TradingView lineage — vivid blue for action, blue-tinted charcoal for surfaces, and the canonical bull/bear pair (teal up, coral down). Tape Gold is the DataLens twist: a warm accent for evidence and data highlights that keeps the palette from reading as a generic trading clone. Semantic colours follow PRD §6 statuses and §12 signal severity.

Primary — Signal Blue #2962ff

CTAs, focus rings, active navigation, chart strokes, links. --accent* role tokens sit on top of this ramp so each theme can retune contrast independently.

3Buttons & Interactive States

Every interactive element shows rest, hover, active, and focus-visible. Hover deepens the shade (never brightens), active adds a 1px translate, focus uses a visible ring for keyboard accessibility.

Variants

Primary = one per view (the main next step: "Generate snapshot", "Ask a question"). Secondary = supporting action. Outline = tertiary. Ghost = low-emphasis utility. Semantic variants are reserved for explicit confirm/deny.

Gold & ink pairing

Tape Gold is the one accent that cannot take white text — it fails contrast. Gold surfaces always take near-black ink (--on-secondary: #1a1203). This is deliberate: it is the detail that makes the system feel like a terminal rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

Concept explainer (PRD §16)

Free Cash Flow declined 18%
Hover or click the metric for the chosen depth
Quick Standard Deep
Standard: Free cash flow shows how much cash the business generates after spending on things such as property and equipment.
Deep: FCF = OCF − CapEx = ₦210B − ₦95B = ₦115B (2025). Declined 18% from ₦140B (2024) because capex rose ₦38B while OCF fell. Signal: rising capital intensity without matching cash generation. p.52 · p.58
